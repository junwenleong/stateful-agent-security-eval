Changes in v6 (relative to arXiv:2605.08442v5)

Scope and framing
- The abstract and introduction are rescoped. The injection-execution dissociation is now
  stated as a bidirectional claim (blocking injection does not imply blocking execution, and
  vice versa) that is evidenced across the frontier set, where some vendors block predominantly
  at storage and others predominantly at execution. The open-source factorial is stated to
  evidence only the execution-stage half, because its models are pre-selected Vulnerable
  Executors with storage saturated at 100 percent.
- Attack success (ASR) is defined explicitly as exfiltration to the attacker address in any
  session of a run, not only in the intended trigger session S4. This makes attack-chain
  collapses (early sends under sandbox, intervening-session re-injection) count as successes
  rather than being silently missed.

Reproducibility disclosure
- The primary April 2026 factorial is disclosed as having been collected on a single long-lived
  Ollama 0.20.6 daemon (OLLAMA_MAX_LOADED_MODELS=1, 2-minute keep-alive that unloads but does
  not fully restart between models). The fresh-daemon-per-model protocol was adopted only for
  the later RATG and judge-scaling extensions, after the daemon-state artefact was identified.
- The qwq:32b defence-inversion result is demoted from a headline finding to an
  environment-fragile observation: its baseline did not reproduce on a freshly booted machine,
  and the reusable contribution is the bypass mechanism (RAG-fallback reconstruction), which
  replicates cross-family, rather than the single-model inversion.

Corrected and refined numbers
- Prompt Hardening is reported at 77.8 percent ASR overall, with the per-arm figures 87.5
  percent and 100 percent stated alongside, replacing the previous single summary figure.
- The coding-agent dissociation table now reports 691 runs (N >= 50 per cell) with a per-run
  joint Dissociation percentage, defined as the fraction of runs where the model both detects
  the injection and executes it, rather than min(Detect percent, Execute percent).
- The GPT-4o standalone disclosure figure under standard compliance framing is 12.5 percent
  ASR (rising to 60.3 percent under authority escalation).

Defence framing
- RATG is retitled and scoped as a proof-of-concept existence result (a content-layer
  intervention resolves the schema-layer dilemma), not an adversarially robust defence.
- The "iatrogenic" framing of tool-schema restriction is retained but scoped to the tested
  models and configurations rather than asserted as a general property.

Disclosure hygiene
- All institutional gateway routing prefixes were removed from the model-identifier appendix.
  The provenance table now records the bare requested model identifiers, and the sentence that
  described a gateway prefix as an aliasing artefact is removed. This is the primary reason a
  replacement is issued: v5 carried these prefixes in the appendix.

Dialect
- British English throughout the prose, consistent with the author's other papers. Code
  identifiers, condition names, file names and the title are unchanged.
